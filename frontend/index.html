💡 Understand Duval Triangle in 30 Seconds
Simple Guide

The Duval Triangle analyzes only 3 key gases dissolved in transformer oil. Each gas acts as a unique chemical "fingerprint" of internal thermal or electrical events:

🔵 Methane (CH₄) — "Lukewarm / Corona Gas" 53.3%

Forms when oil is gently warmed (<150°C) or microscopic electric buzzing (corona). Very low immediate danger.

🟡 Ethylene (C₂H₄) — "Hot Metal Gas" 46.7%

Forms when copper wires or iron core run extremely hot (300°C–700°C). Indicates continuous heavy overload or failed cooling pumps.

🔴 Acetylene (C₂H₂) — "Lightning Arc Gas" 0.0%

Forms ONLY during violent electric lightning arcs inside oil (>1000°C). Indicates breakdown of insulation and catastrophic explosion risk!
